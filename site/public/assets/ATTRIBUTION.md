# Scene texture

- File: `rocky-terrain.jpg` (unaltered 1K diffuse texture)
- Asset: [Rocky Terrain](https://polyhaven.com/a/rocky_terrain)
- Author: Amal Kumar / Poly Haven
- License: [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license)
- Download: https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/rocky_terrain/rocky_terrain_diff_1k.jpg

This texture is projected onto the generated 3D landscape. It is not a scene screenshot or a page background. Other landscape meshes, foliage, bridges, and pavilions are generated by this repository.

Additional unaltered 1K JPG maps from the same Rocky Terrain asset, author and CC0 license:

- `rocky-terrain-height.jpg`: https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/rocky_terrain/rocky_terrain_disp_1k.jpg
- `rocky-terrain-arm.jpg`: https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/rocky_terrain/rocky_terrain_arm_1k.jpg

These provide surface relief, ambient occlusion and roughness. The maps are hosted locally; their MD5 checksums were verified against the official Poly Haven API at download.
